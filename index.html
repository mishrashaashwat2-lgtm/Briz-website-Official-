What is Briz?

Briz is a personal AI assistant created by TrueAI. The goal is to build an AI experience that feels useful, simple, and personal — helping users with everyday questions, learning, creativity, organization, and productivity.

Briz is intended to become an AI companion that can assist users across different tasks, instead of requiring separate tools for everything.
